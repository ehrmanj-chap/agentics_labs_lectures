The lunch plan meets reality

One conversation. New facts. A few reasons to pause.

These are prepared example responses, not a live AI connection. For your on-screen chatbot walkthrough, use the instructor cookbook. Several different menus can work.

Ask the room

Which new fact changed the plan? What still needs checking? When do we have enough evidence to stop planning?

Listen for reasoning about the constraints. Exact phrasing and a single “correct” menu are not the goal.
