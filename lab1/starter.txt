Lab 1 — Plan the club lunch

This is a problem brief, not a prompt to copy. Use your own words with your chatbot.

Your student club is serving lunch tomorrow at noon. Use a chatbot to help make a workable plan as the facts change. You choose what to ask and how to explain the problem.

You expect 24 people at tomorrow’s noon lunch. The supplier offers chicken sandwiches for $6, vegetarian wraps for $5, and drinks for $1. Delivery costs $12 for the whole order. Your total budget is $200; all prices include taxes and fees.

Work with your chatbot to propose an order. Decide what information it needs and what you would want to check before committing. You can ask questions, challenge an answer, or revise your request.
