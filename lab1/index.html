Lab 1 · 30 minutes

Human Agent Loop

Use a bot of your choice to plan a museum order. You provide the tool results and check whether its plan still makes sense.

Work in pairs or groups of 3–4: bot driver, tool operator, and auditor. Combine roles as needed. Solo is fine too.

Open PantherAI
Chapman users: choose Chapman SSO Login and sign in with your school account. Visiting students can join a partner or use an existing free chat account. If the platform is down, use the scripted demo as your conversation.
0–5 MINUTES

Pick a bot. Meet the museum.

Open a new chat. Record the full model name in your worksheet. Copy the starter below. The museum, dates, stock and prices are fictional.

Starter message
You are the planning component in a classroom simulation for a tiny museum. A school wants 24 educational prints delivered by October 15. Today is October 1 in this fictional scenario. Total budget: $240 including shipping. Product preference: River print. Any substitute requires customer approval. Spending over $200 requires manager approval.

You have no connected tools in this exercise. I play the tool operator and supply observations. Available simulated tools: LOOKUP_INVENTORY, CALCULATE_QUOTE, REQUEST_APPROVAL, DRAFT_REPLY.

For each turn, briefly report GOAL, CURRENT STATE, NEXT ACTION + TOOL, EXPECTED OBSERVATION, and STOP / ESCALATE status. Choose one next action and wait for my tool result. Treat unobserved facts as unknown. Keep earlier confirmed facts unless I update them. Do not claim to send, buy, reserve, or complete a real transaction. Stop when the quote is ready with required approvals recorded, or escalate if the constraints cannot be met.

Begin with the next action you need. Keep the response under 150 words.
5–10 MINUTES

Label the first turn

Find its goal, current state, next action, tool and stop condition. Which facts does it actually know? Has it claimed to do anything it cannot do?

You are simulating tools. A sentence saying “order placed” is not a transaction receipt.

10–22 MINUTES

Let the world interrupt

Reveal one card at a time. Paste it into the same conversation, wait for the response, and record what changed. If the bot needs a calculation result, calculate it yourself and supply it. Allow roughly three minutes per card.

Tool result 1 · Initial inventory

LOOKUP_INVENTORY result: River prints cost $8 each, with 30 available. Garden prints cost $7 each, with 40 available. Standard delivery takes 5 calendar days from today and costs $15 per order. The budget includes delivery. Calculate a quote, but do not send or purchase anything.

Observation 2 · Stock changed

UPDATE: The inventory system corrected its count. Only 12 River prints are available. Garden stock is still 40. The customer still needs 24 prints. What state changes, and what should happen next?

Observation 3 · Deadline moved

UPDATE: The customer now needs delivery by October 4. Standard delivery still takes 5 calendar days. Rush delivery takes 2 calendar days and costs $45 per order instead of $15. Today is still October 1. Any substitute needs customer approval and totals over $200 need manager approval. What can be done, and what must wait?

Observation 4 · Approvals arrived

REQUEST_APPROVAL result: The customer accepts 24 Garden prints. The manager approves the $213 total including rush delivery. Use the 2-calendar-day rush option. Draft a short customer reply and state the final stop condition. No real order has been placed.

If it makes a mistake, save the evidence and state what the system should have done. You do not need to force a perfect run.

22–28 MINUTES

Make the loop visible

Finish the worksheet. Draw or describe how the latest observation changed the next action. Include at least two observations and a stop or escalation condition.

28–30 MINUTES

Compare with a neighbor

What made the system agentic? Where did human judgment enter? If time remains, try the same starter in a second model and compare the first action.
