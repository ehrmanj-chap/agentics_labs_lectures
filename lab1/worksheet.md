# Lab 1 — Plan the club lunch

Human Agent Loop · 20 minutes · Jordan Ehrman / Riverbot Agentics

Your student club is serving lunch tomorrow at noon. Use a chatbot to help make a workable plan as the facts change. You choose what to ask and how to explain the problem.

Work individually or in pairs. Open one new conversation in a chatbot of your choice and keep using it for all four parts. Record the app and model name. Read each part when you reach it; spend about five minutes on each.

Each person needs one meal and one drink. The total budget is $200. All listed prices include taxes and fees. This is a fictional planning exercise; no actual order is placed.

**Name(s), app and model:**

## Part 1 · Make a first plan (0–5 minutes)

You expect 24 people at tomorrow’s noon lunch. The supplier offers chicken sandwiches for $6, vegetarian wraps for $5, and drinks for $1. Delivery costs $12 for the whole order. Your total budget is $200; all prices include taxes and fees.

Work with your chatbot to propose an order. Decide what information it needs and what you would want to check before committing. You can ask questions, challenge an answer, or revise your request.

**Your record:** Record your proposed quantities and total. What is still unknown?

## Part 2 · The guest list changes (5–10 minutes)

There will now be 30 people, including 8 vegetarians. The supplier has only 18 chicken sandwiches, but enough vegetarian wraps and drinks. The total budget is still $200. Everyone still needs one meal and one drink.

Bring the new facts into the same conversation and work out a revised order. Check the arithmetic and the earlier requirements yourself. Do you need to change more than just the headcount?

**Your record:** Record the revised quantities and total. What changed, and did the chatbot remember the other constraints?

[Write here.]

## Part 3 · Delivery falls through (10–15 minutes)

The supplier’s earliest delivery is 12:30 p.m., after the noon lunch starts. Pickup is available at 11:15 a.m. and removes the $12 delivery fee. A club member might collect the order, but they have not confirmed.

Explore a workable proposal with the chatbot. Decide what you can settle now and what still needs a person to confirm. Keep the headcount, dietary needs, stock and budget in view.

**Your record:** Record a provisional plan and the confirmation it still needs. Has the chatbot treated a possibility as a promise?

[Write here.]

## Part 4 · Close the loop (15–20 minutes)

The club member confirms that they can collect the order at 11:15 a.m. and bring it to the lunch before noon.

Finish a short proposal with the meal and drink quantities, total cost, and pickup arrangement. Check it against all the facts. Then look back at the conversation: what did the chatbot do well, and what did you have to check or correct?

**Your record:** Record the final proposal, one thing the chatbot did well, one check or correction you made, and why the planning task is finished.

[Write here.]

Save your four short records and the app/model name. A few sentences per part are enough. Multiple menus can work; explain your choices. A useful correction counts as evidence of learning.

**No chatbot?** If you cannot use a chatbot, work with a partner: one proposes a plan and the other checks it. Use the four parts in order and record the same decisions. Compare with the prepared demo after you finish.
